# Brand Name Options

## Option 1: Loop Zero

**Tagline:** Repetitive work, automated out.

**Positioning:**
Loop Zero finds the loops in your business, the email you send 40 times a week, the report you rebuild every Monday, the invoice you re-enter into three systems. We automate them out. Built for Bangkok and ASEAN small businesses running on talent but bleeding time on admin. No "digital transformation" theater. Just fewer things for your team to do by hand.

**Why it works:**
"Loop" is a word every business owner understands without a technical explanation. "Zero" makes the promise concrete and binary: the task either ends or it doesn't. The name works in English, doesn't require translation, and is short enough for a domain, a logo, and a WhatsApp business name.

---

## Option 2: Sahai

**Tagline:** We handle the boring stuff.

**Positioning:**
Sahai (ช่วย) means "to help" in Thai. That's the entire pitch. Your front desk stops re-typing appointments. Your sales team stops copying leads into a spreadsheet. Your ops lead stops chasing unpaid invoices at 9pm. We build the bots and workflows that do it for them. Local team, local language, built for how Thai and ASEAN businesses actually run.

**Why it works:**
The name is warm and local. Thai speakers immediately get "we help you." Non-Thai speakers get a short, pronounceable word that sticks. The tagline avoids "AI" and "automation" entirely, which keeps non-technical readers comfortable. It positions you as a helper, not a vendor selling software.

---

## Option 3: Handoff

**Tagline:** People for people. Bots for busywork.

**Positioning:**
Every hour your staff spends on data entry is an hour they're not with a customer, a patient, or a client. Handoff builds the automation layer that takes the non-human tasks off your team's plate. We set it up in days, not months, and your people get that time back. The name describes exactly what happens: work that used to sit with a person gets handed off to a system.

**Why it works:**
One word, no explanation needed. Any manager in any industry gets the concept instantly. It frames the value as "your team gets time back" rather than "we deploy AI." The tagline is punchy and works in both English and Thai contexts. It also scales naturally: you hand off more work over time, which maps to a retainer model.
